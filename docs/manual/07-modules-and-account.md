# Modules and Account

## Modules

![Modules](img/modules.png)

Administrators only. Every container that writes to InfluxDB appears here as a data module
with its measurements, whether it is connected and when it last wrote. It is the quickest way to
see that the collector, the balancer and Foresight are alive and producing data for the selected
cluster, and to explore the raw measurements without a query tool.

## Account

![Account](img/account.png)

Your own account: your name, your email address, your password, and two-factor authentication
with a time-based one-time password app. Two-factor applies to your account only; an administrator
can reset it for you if you lose the device. This page is the same for administrators and users.

To sign in with another address, enter it with your current password: a link goes to the new
address, and your login changes once you open it (within 24 hours). Forgot your password? Use the
link on the sign-in page: a link to choose a new one is mailed to you (it works for 60 minutes).
